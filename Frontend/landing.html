Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about GreenRoute.

We verify users through email or SMS, and for pilots, require .edu or company emails. All trips can be shared with friends/family via live links, and pickup locations snap to nearby landmarks to protect privacy.

Users typically save 40-70% on commuting costs by splitting fuel and parking expenses. The exact savings depend on your route and how many riders you share with.

You can cancel or modify your trip up to 30 minutes before departure. We'll automatically notify matched riders and suggest alternative options when available.

Our algorithm considers route proximity, timing compatibility, and user preferences. It prioritizes matches that minimize detours while maximizing convenience for all parties.
