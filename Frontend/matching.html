ℹ️ How It Works
  • Choose your role (Rider or Driver) and click the card above
  • Enter your name and phone number
  • Select your current location and destination on the map
  • For drivers: Set maximum detour distance you're willing to take
  • Our algorithm finds compatible matches based on proximity
  • Contact matched users directly via phone or WhatsApp
  • All data is stored locally in text files for demo purposes
